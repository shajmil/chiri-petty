Chiri Party asset credits

Original procedural stage, characters, avatars, logo and prompt text: created for this project.

Sound library: short clips from public GitHub meme soundboards (3kh0/soundboard, PareekshithPalat/IDE-Error-Soundboard,
evansuong/Meme-Soundboard-Webpage, Fazalhasann/Fazals-Meme-SoundBoard, bytevictor/FAAAAH-meme-sound-multiplatform,
jimmytop/soundmachine, EmanuelJrc/WeeBoard), Wikimedia Commons, and Malayalam film clips the project owner saved
from Myinstants pages (film audio with no licence). Every clip's source repository and path, the licence
that repository declares, the source and final file hashes and the edits made are listed in audio/library/CREDITS.json.
A repository's licence covers what its authors own; many of these clips originate from films, games and other products
whose owners have not licensed them here. Rights holders who want a clip removed can identify it by its source path.
